Projects

Each project follows the same outline: the problem, what I did, and how it turned out. The newest is first, and I add to this page as I build more.

HyTech Racing electrical onboarding

Georgia Tech Formula SAE electric team, fall 2026.

The problem

HyTech asks every new electrical member to prove they can design a board and write firmware before they touch the car. I started with no experience in either tool.

What I did

Designed a keychain PCB in Altium, from schematic through layout. Then wrote firmware in PlatformIO for a temperature-sensing light.

The outcome

A team lead checked off the PCB on October 8, and I finished the firmware project after it. I'm now designing a brake light system for the car.

Firmware onboarding code on GitHub

Robot Tour autonomous robot

Science Olympiad, Quince Orchard High School, 2024 to 2026.

The problem

Build a robot that drives itself through a randomized obstacle course, passes through gates for points, parks on a target zone, and finishes close to a target time.

What I did

Designed and built the robot with a partner for tight turning. It drifted off line, so I wrote stabilization and self-correction code to hold its heading.

The outcome

First place at the UMBC tournament and seventh at the Maryland state tournament.

FTC competition robot

FIRST Tech Challenge, Quince Orchard High School Robotics Club, 2022 to 2026.

The problem

Each season FIRST Tech Challenge releases a new game, and teams have a few months to design, build and program a robot to play it.

What I did

Led the team through design, coding and assembly. Modeled and simulated prototypes in Onshape and Fusion 360, and 3D printed custom parts to FTC specifications. Mentored newer members in my last year.

The outcome

Regional semifinals as team lead, and the THINK Engineering Notebook Award the following season.
