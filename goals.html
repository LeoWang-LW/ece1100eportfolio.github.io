Career goals

I want to build autonomous and robotic systems that make technology cleaner, and to lead the teams that build them.

Where I'm headed

In five years
Working as an engineer developing autonomous and robotic systems, with at least one research experience and one co-op or internship behind me.
In ten years
A researcher or technical lead on technology that improves people's lives, with sustainability as the measure of whether it did.

Why these threads

Georgia Tech's electrical engineering degree is built from two threads. Mine are Robotics and Autonomous Systems and Circuit Technology. They go hand in hand: to build more advanced robots, you also need to be able to design the circuit boards that power the motors and run the control. I'm keeping Bioengineering open as a third option because I'm curious how robotics can help in medicine.

How I get from here to there

  1. Year 1, 2026 to 2027

    Earn real responsibility on HyTech Racing, starting with the brake light system. Join a Vertically Integrated Projects team for a first research experience. Join Silicon Jackets in the spring to learn chip design. Keep doing environmental work and advocacy. Land a summer 2027 internship in robotics or hardware.

  2. Year 2, 2027 to 2028

    Start the robotics thread courses in signals and control. Continue research, and take a leadership position in at least one club that supports my professional goals. Study abroad if the schedule allows.

  3. Year 3, 2028 to 2029

    Complete a co-op in a field I'm considering, to test it before committing. Carry more of the electrical design on HyTech and in Silicon Jackets.

  4. Year 4, 2029 to 2030

    Finish both threads with the extra ECE electives my AP credit makes room for, then graduate in May 2030 and decide between industry and graduate research.

What I still need to find out

I have never done research, so I don't yet know whether I like it. My picture of what robotics engineers do day to day is also limited. Before I fully commit, I want to talk to people in the field and try the work myself. That is what the VIP team, the internship and the co-op are for.
